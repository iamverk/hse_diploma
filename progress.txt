## Discovered Patterns
- Clothing products in corpus map cleanly to broad apparel groupings (gendered, children, footwear, accessories).
- No local products.jsonl; use data/experiments/*/start_taxonomy.json as domain priors
- Atomic stories (1 domain, 1 level) prevent timeouts
- check_edge.py --batch is faster than individual calls
- Wu-Palmer CSC has ceiling for domains with poor WordNet coverage (Pet Products, Cosmetics)
- Using a root label that matches corpus vocabulary (e.g., "Products") improves root-level NLIV
- Smartphones, laptops, phones, TVs, cameras, and headphones all show strong Electronics partitionability in corpus text
- Home Goods text clusters into clear groups for decor, storage, furniture, improvement, and cleaning products.
